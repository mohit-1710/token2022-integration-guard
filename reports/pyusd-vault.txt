  HANDLE  TransferFeeConfig [high]
        impact:     A fee is withheld from every transfer, so the amount credited to your account is LESS than the amount sent.

        detail:     feeBasisPoints=0 (0.00%), maximumFee=0 — currently zero, but the fee authority can raise it
